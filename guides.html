Bounty Guide

Bounties in One Piece increase based on threat to the World Government, not just strength. Each raise comes after major actions, exposure, or influence shifts.

  • ◆ Defeating Major Figures: Beating Warlords, commanders, or strong opponents massively boosts bounty.
  • ◆ Attacking the Government: Actions like destroying Enies Lobby, attacking Celestial Dragons, or prison breaks spike bounty.
  • ◆ Large-Scale Crimes: War involvement, mass destruction, or freeing criminals increases perceived danger.
  • ◆ Reputation & Influence: Leading crews, alliances, or being seen as a major figure raises bounty.
  • ◆ New Information Revealed: Hidden strength, abilities, or lineage becoming known increases bounty.
Important Notes:
  • ◆ Threat > Strength: Bounties reflect danger to the world, not raw power.
  • ◆ Can Be Delayed/Hidden: Some feats don’t increase bounty if covered up.
  • ◆ Can Jump Drastically: Major events can cause huge sudden increases.
(BIG ACTIONS = BIG BOUNTY JUMPS)

Limit Request Guide

Limits regulate access to powerful races, rare traits, and unique abilities. Approval focuses on planning and concept quality, not raw power.

  • ◆ Concept Clarity: Clearly define what you’re requesting.
  • ◆ Strong Concept: Unique, well thought out idea.
  • ◆ Planning Ability: Show how you’ll use and develop it.
  • ◆ Lore Fit: Explain how it fits the world.
  • ◆ Originality: Avoid copy-paste ideas.
  • ◆ Progression Path: Show growth over time.
  • ◆ Consistency: Keep the idea logical and coherent.
  • ◆ Role Impact: Adds to interaction and RP.
  • ◆ Concise Justification: Keep it short and structured.
Bad Examples (What NOT To Do):
  • ◆ Verbose Yap: "This allows deeper connections..." → No how/why.
  • ◆ Empty Statements: "It makes my character stronger." → Says nothing.
  • ◆ Hype Only: "This is really cool and unique." → Not a justification.
  • ◆ Promises: "I’ll balance it later." → Doesn’t matter.
  • ◆ Vague Use: "I’ll use it creatively." → No detail.
  • ◆ Copy Idea: "Like X but slightly different." → No originality.
  • ◆ No Reasoning: "It just fits the lore." → Explain how.
  • ◆ Wall of Text: Unstructured = ignored.
  • ◆ Too Basic: "Strong race with good abilities." → No depth.
  • ◆ Personal Want: "I just want it." → Not valid.
Important Notes:
  • ◆ NO WALLS OF TEXT: Use ~10 points max.
  • ◆ WHY + HOW > STATEMENTS
  • ◆ CONCEPT > POWER
(PLANNING + CONCEPT = APPROVAL)

Character Submission Guide

Character submissions are judged on concept, writing quality, and execution. Strong OCs follow a clear identity and feel like they belong in the world.

  • ◆ Clear Identity: Your character must have a defined role (swordsman, doctor, king, etc).
  • ◆ Concrete Concept: Keep it simple and specific, not vague or overloaded.
  • ◆ Goal / Dream: Every character needs a clear ambition driving them forward.
  • ◆ Background: Show where they come from and what shaped them.
  • ◆ Personality: Defined traits that affect how they act in RP.
  • ◆ Consistency: Everything must align (no random traits thrown together).
  • ◆ Flaws / Conflict: Internal or external struggles that drive development.
  • ◆ Effort & Structure: Clean, readable, and clearly written.
Writing Standard (IMPORTANT):
  • ◆ Write Like Oda: Keep concepts simple but meaningful, not overcomplicated.
  • ◆ Strong Core Trait: Build the character around one strong idea (ex: swordsman aiming for the top).
  • ◆ Personality First: Let traits and quirks define how they feel in RP.
  • ◆ Show Through Action: Don’t just state traits—make them evident in behavior.
Important Notes:
  • ◆ NO LAZY OCS: Low-effort or generic submissions will be denied.
  • ◆ NO OVERLOADING: Don’t stack random ideas; keep it focused.
  • ◆ QUALITY > COMPLEXITY: Simple, well-written characters are better than messy ones.
(CLEAR CONCEPT + GOOD WRITING = APPROVAL)

Devil Fruit Guide

Devil Fruits are built on clear concepts and structured abilities. Each fruit must follow a defined theme with perks that match its type.

Core Requirements:
  • ◆ Clear Concept: One defined ability (ex: lightning, limbs, dragon).
  • ◆ Correct Classification: Paramecia, Logia, or Zoan must be accurate.
  • ◆ Consistent Theme: All perks must follow the same concept.
  • ◆ Structured Tiers: Abilities scale and unlock progressively.
  • ◆ Concrete Abilities: No vague effects—everything must be defined.
Required Perk Types (Pick & Define Clearly):
  • ◆ Primary Ability: The main function (ex: create, control, transform).
  • ◆ Offensive Tool: A direct way to attack (projectiles, strikes, effects).
  • ◆ Utility / Mobility: Travel, reach, or controlling the field space.
  • ◆ Passive Effect: Constant bonus (sensing, resistance, stat boost).
  • ◆ Limitation / Locked Mechanic: Restricted at Tier 1 (full form, intangibility).
Ability Guidelines:
  • ◆ Each perk must include clear numbers or effects (%, turns, limits).
  • ◆ Keep abilities simple and direct, not overloaded.
  • ◆ Every perk must connect to the fruit concept.
  • ◆ No vague wording like “enhanced” without defining how.
  • ◆ Tier 1 is basic only—advanced abilities come later.
Important Notes:
  • ◆ NO RANDOM PERKS: Everything must match the fruit theme.
  • ◆ STRUCTURE > HYPE: Clean, readable abilities get approved.
  • ◆ KEEP IT GROUNDED: Don’t overload Tier 1 with high-level abilities.
(CLEAR CONCEPT + 5 DEFINED PERKS = APPROVAL)

Fighting Style Guide

Fighting Styles are built on structured techniques and clear scaling. They must feel impactful, balanced, and situational, not spam-based.

Core Requirements:
  • ◆ Defined Style Identity: Clear concept (brawler, swordsman, assassin, etc).
  • ◆ Skill Cap: Maximum 8 skills total.
  • ◆ Weighted Skills: Each skill must have clear effects and scaling (%, turns, conditions).
  • ◆ No Passive Spam: Skills must be activated or situational, not always on.
  • ◆ Consistency: All skills must match the same fighting style concept.
Skill Rules:
  • ◆ 1 Core Skill: The only consistently usable ability (basic, repeatable).
  • ◆ Situational Skills: All other skills must require conditions, triggers, or limits.
  • ◆ Clear Effects: Every skill must state exact buffs, effects, or outcomes.
  • ◆ No Overlapping: Avoid duplicate effects across multiple skills.
  • ◆ Balanced Utility: Mix offense, defense, mobility, and reaction.
Tier Progression:

Tier 1 — Novice: Unlock 1 core OR 1 situational skill. Techniques scale to D-Rank (1.5x).

Tier 2 — Apprentice: Unlock 1 situational skill. Techniques scale to C-Rank (2x).

Tier 3 — Intermediate: Unlock 1–2 situational skills. Techniques scale to B-Rank (3x).

Tier 4 — Advanced: Unlock 1–2 situational skills. Techniques scale to A-Rank (4x) + improved effects.

Tier 5 — Mastery: Unlock final skill (max 8). Techniques scale to S-Rank (5x) peak efficiency.

Important Notes:
  • ◆ NO ALL-ACTIVE BUILDS: Only 1 consistent skill, rest situational.
  • ◆ KEEP SKILLS IMPACTFUL: Each ability must matter.
  • ◆ NO OVERLOADING: Don't stack too many effects in one skill.
  • ◆ STRUCTURE MATTERS: Clean formatting = easier approval.
(CLEAR STYLE + WEIGHTED SKILLS = APPROVAL)

Technique Guide

Techniques are the core of combat, built on rank scaling, clear effects, and proper limits. Each technique must follow its rank boundaries strictly.

Technique Rules:
  • ◆ Follow Rank Scaling: Every technique must stay within its rank multiplier.
  • ◆ No Overstacking: Effects cannot exceed the max value of the rank.
  • ◆ Clear Buff Type: Specify if it boosts 1 stat or multiple stats.
  • ◆ Defined Effects: No vague wording—state exact % / turns / impact.
  • ◆ Cooldown Respect: Follow universal cooldown structure per rank.
Rank System:

F Rank: (1–1.5x) ➟ Basic framework techniques.

D Rank: (2–2.5x) ➟ Starter techniques, 1 stat only.

C Rank: (2–2.5x) ➟ Multi-stat or improved utility.

B Rank: (3–3.5x) ➟ Strong, impactful techniques.

A Rank: (4–4.5x) ➟ High-level, fight-defining abilities.

S Rank: (5x) ➟ Peak techniques, once per battle.

Scaling Logic:
  • ◆ Upper Limit = Single Stat Buff
  • ◆ Lower Limit = Multi Stat Split
Support / Duration Rule:
Value can be distributed over time.
Example: 1.1x Speed for 5 turns ≈ 1.5x for 1 turn
Important Notes:
  • ◆ NO EXCEEDING LIMITS
  • ◆ KEEP IT WITHIN REASON OF YOUR OWN FIGHTING STYLE!
  • ◆ COOLDOWNS ARE UNIVERSAL
(FOLLOW RANKS + CLEAR SCALING = VALID TECHNIQUE)

Inventions / Craft Guide

Inventions and crafts are structured player-created items, devices, or economic systems. They provide functional or narrative benefits and must follow a clear cost and feasibility framework.

Core Requirements:
  • ◆ Defined Purpose: Each invention or craft must have a clear function (combat, utility, exploration, or economy).
  • Sub-Type Classification:
    • Recipe / Drugs ➟ Consumables with effects.
    • Ships ➟ Vessels for travel or trade.
    • Economy / Businesses ➟ Trade, shops, services, or production.
    • Weapons ➟ Offensive tools for combat.
    • Equipment ➟ Tools, gadgets, or utilities.
    • Suits / Armor / Specialized Gear ➟ Wearable enhancements.
  • ◆ Concrete Effects: Must clearly define what the invention does, including limitations and mechanics.
  • ◆ Cost & Feasibility: Every invention must bear a legitimate cost, reflecting materials, labor, or market value. Costs should be determined by finding relevant items from the shop (cost finding).
  • ◆ Progression / Upgrades: Items can be upgraded, improved, or mass-produced, but each iteration must have a recalculated cost.
Guidelines for Submission:
  • ◆ Consistency: Effects, cost, and sub-type must align logically.
  • ◆ Narrative Fit: Inventions should make sense for the setting or island economy.
  • ◆ Approval Required: Staff must review inventions for cost validity, balance, and feasibility.
  • ◆ Documentation: Include a recipe list, material requirements, or workflow to justify cost and practicality.
(COST FINDING + LOGICAL FUNCTION = APPROVAL)

Beast Guide

Beasts are structured by tier, traits, and adaptations. Each beast must follow a clear classification and fit within the ecosystem or narrative.

Core Requirements:
  • ◆ Defined Traits: Each beast must have its traits clearly described (ex: venomous bite, camouflage).
  • ◆ Appropriate Tier: Domestic, Savage, or Alpha; ensure power and rarity match the tier.
  • ◆ Environmental Fit: The beast should make sense for the island or setting it inhabits.
  • ◆ Progression / Training: Beasts can be trained; tamers grow bonds and abilities over time.
  • ◆ Concrete Descriptions: Explain how and why abilities or behaviors work.
Tier Classification & Requirements:

Domestic Creatures (日本国内): Lowest tier, unlimited number. Each has one core trait. Suitable for companionship or minor combat support.

Savage Creatures (野生の生き物): Medium tier, limited/endangered. Each has one core trait + one adaptation for survival or combat enhancement.

Alpha Beasts (アルファ獣): Highest tier, extremely rare. Each has two core traits + two adaptations; formidable and often plot-relevant.

Starting Conditions for Tamers:
  • ◆ One Roll Per Island: Players can encounter or submit only one beast per island.
  • ◆ Initial Bonds: Tamers start with 3 bonds with creatures.
  • ◆ Random Starting DKI: Tamers begin at a staff-assigned DKI; beasts level from there.
Beast Submission Guidelines:
  • ◆ Trait & Adaptation Balance: Must match tier and intended challenge level.
  • ◆ Concrete Behavior: Describe abilities, combat style, and ecological role.
  • ◆ Plot Hooks: Alpha Beasts should have unique lore or narrative relevance.
  • ◆ Approval Required: Submissions may be denied for vague descriptions.
(TIER LOGIC + TRAITS + ADAPTATIONS = APPROVAL)

NPC Guide

NPCs are secondary characters that follow players. They provide support, narrative depth, and minor combat assistance, but have limitations compared to player characters.

Core Requirements:
  • ◆ Acquisition: NPCs must be obtained through RP mechanics (slavery, bondings, etc).
  • ◆ Max Followers: Players may have up to 10 NPCs at any given time.
  • ◆ Mortality: NPCs can die, affecting strategy and requiring careful management.
  • ◆ Progression: NPC growth is halved compared to players and operates on soft cap settings.
  • ◆ DKI Assignment: NPCs use a DKI equal to half of their region’s DKI.
Role & Functionality:
  • ◆ Support Role: NPCs assist in combat, exploration, and narrative interactions.
  • ◆ Bound Mechanics: Bonds may enhance loyalty, combat efficiency, or unlock interactions.
  • ◆ Limited Autonomy: NPCs act based on soft cap stats and won't exceed tier limits.
Acquisition & Management Guidelines:
  • ◆ Ethical & Narrative Considerations: All acquisitions must be staff-approved.
  • ◆ Replacement & Loss: Dead NPCs can be replaced, but previous progress is not recoverable.
  • ◆ Integration: NPCs can interact with beasts and islands under soft cap rules.
(NPC ACQUISITION + SOFT CAP LIMITS = APPROVAL)
