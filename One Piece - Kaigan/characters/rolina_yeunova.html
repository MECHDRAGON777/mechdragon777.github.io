[ Personal Details ]
# Personality

A quite cheerful girl who has taken a knack for the weather due to being raised in Weatheria. She seems to value her speed more so than her strength and is keen to read people quite carefully after her first reactions to the native Weatherians. She has always been one to observe first before striking when given the opportunity.

# Appearance

Rolina is a Snowshoe Rabbit Mink of slender build and of mid-back level hair length dyed blue with violetish-pink ends. She wears a cloak that is made of conductive materials laced inside.

Aspiration(s): Aquire and protect all Weather-based Devil Fruit.

Likes: Destructive Weather, Cold | Dislikes: Heat

Goal:

  • Aquire every Weather-Fruit.
  • Learn more about her birthplace.

[ Backstory ]

𝐂𝐇𝐑𝐎𝐍𝐈𝐂𝐋𝐄𝐒 𝐎𝐅 𝐓𝐇𝐄 𝐑𝐀𝐁𝐁𝐈𝐓

Arc I: The Sky-Bound Kit (Ages 2-5)

At age two, Rolina stumbled upon a Weatherian Balloon Ship and was not found until a day after Weatheria had passed Zou. With no control over the Sky Island's direction, the Weatherians took the young Mink in. While she grew to love her home in the sky, she occasionally longed for her home island’s nature, which led to her refusal to abandon the speech patterns associated with her tribe, even though she was too young to remember her biological parents.
By age four, she became interested in the Weatherians' work, learning that the climate she experienced was artificial. Around this time, she realized she possessed Electro due to her Mink heritage. The Weatherians taught her basic technology and how to hold writing apparatus; however, she was never taught to read proper words. Consequently, she developed a unique writing system that only she could read as she grew older.

Arc II: Moonlight and Mastery (Ages 5-7)

Around her sixth birthday, Rolina accidentally witnessed the moon rise and awakened her Sulong form for the first time. She rampaged briefly before passing out in a shadow. From then on, she was taught to avoid moonlight. As she hit the six-year mark, she began developing her Electro further. A Weatherian gifted her a staff for self-defense, noting her strikes were "electrifying" and mentioning the existence of mystical Devil Fruits.
By age seven, she became more hesitant to attack during training, focusing instead on defensive parries. Curiously, she never realized the weapon she had been given was not a typical Bo-Staff but a Climatact. The Weatherians hoped that one day she would finally unlock the potential they had spent years passing down to her.

Arc III: The Copper Cape and Fantasies (Ages 10-12)

Rolina lost her original Climatact at age ten and replaced it with a metal staff. She wasn't hindered by the change since she hadn't learned the Climatact's true functions anyway. At age twelve, she adopted her trademark copper-colored cape. She believed the metal would help her redirect attacks using Electro, though it never actually worked; she kept it mostly because she thought it looked cool.
She also began fantasizing about weather-oriented Devil Fruits like the Rumble-Rumble, Snow-Snow, and Gas-Gas fruits. Because her Mink blood made her suffer in hot climates, she longed for the Snow-Snow fruit most of all. During this time, she began chronicling her life in her free time using her personal code.

Arc IV: Atmospheric Readings (Age 14)

At age fourteen, Rolina began learning how to read the weather, which delighted her. She attempted to embed a barometer into insulated gloves to read atmospheric pressure, but due to her lack of field training, she nearly destroyed the instrument. Regardless, she meticulously documented these lessons and her findings in her notebook.

Model Classification: Special Paramecia (Substance Generation, Environmental Manipulation)

Description: A fruit that weaponizes vocal resonance, allowing the user to generate physical musical notes and "carve" a space-time dome known as the Sing-World.

Weakness:

  • ⦿ Standard Devil Fruit weaknesses apply (Seastone/Water).

  • ⦿ Willpower Gap: Targets 50% stronger than the user are immune to environmental manipulation.
  • ⦿ Sensory Immunity: A Deaf target is immune to the Fruit's Environmental Manipulation abilities.
