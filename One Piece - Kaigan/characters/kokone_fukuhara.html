[ Backstory ]

𝐂𝐇𝐑𝐎𝐍𝐈𝐂𝐋𝐄𝐒 𝐎𝐅 𝐓𝐇𝐄 𝐒𝐈𝐋𝐄𝐍𝐓 𝐅𝐋AME

Arc I: The Frozen Scarf of Drum Island

Abandoned as a mere child on the frigid wastes of Drum Island, Kokone’s only connection to her past was the name "Kokone" meticulously sewn into the fabric of her scarf. For two agonizing years, the young girl survived on little more than matches, her tattered scarf, and a desperate will to live. At four years of age, her body finally reached its limit during a brutal winter day; starved and numb, her vision blurred as a blizzard loomed on the horizon.
Before the cold could claim her, she was lifted by a stranger. Collapsing into a warm embrace, she fainted instantly, only to wake up beside a crackling fireplace. It was here she first met ᛖᛊᛏᛖᛚᚨ, whose presence—specifically the flickering flames manifesting behind her—initially intimidated the skittish human girl. Despite the fear, this moment marked the beginning of her new life as part of the Fukuhara family.

Arc II: The Masked Melody of Jaya

Four years passed as Kokone dedicated herself to being a supportive sister and daughter, mastering domestic skills like laundry and cooking to aid her adoptive family. At age eight, she accidentally discovered ᛖᛊᛏᛖᛚᚨ singing in private, sustaining a minor burn when the startled Lunarian's flames flared. Rather than faltering, Kokone saw the light in her sister's eyes and encouraged their father to send them to Jaya so their talents wouldn't go to waste.
Arrival at Jaya was a harsh reality check for the young duo. Inexperienced and penniless, their early shows were mediocre and largely ignored. To cope with her stage fright and find her true voice, Kokone began wearing a mask, performing as the mysterious "Masked Kone." This persona, combined with weekly song releases, eventually catapulted the "Fukuhara Twins" into local stardom.
After five years of carving out a reputation in Jaya's cutthroat music scene, their momentum took a sharp turn. Just as they were finding their rhythm, the Revolutionary Army arrived, identifying the sisters as individuals with the spirit and potential to aid their cause.

Arc III: Paladin of the Revolution

Within the Revolutionary Army, Kokone’s roles were twofold: she refined her culinary talents to nourish her sister and the troops, and she honed a specialized self-defense style. Focusing on immobilization and deflection rather than lethal force, she learned to use the "art of the palm" to neutralize enemies. Her time with the Army also opened her eyes to the deep-seated corruption of the World Government, turning her music into a tool for hope and truth.
After two years of rigorous training, the sisters were dispatched to Water 7 for a final test before becoming official recruits. While ᛖᛊᛏᛖᛚᚨ scouted concert venues, Kokone managed the logistics of their residence. When her sister finally asked if she would join the fight to bring peace to the world, Kokone accepted with a smile, ready to stand by ᛖᛊᛏᛖᛚᚨ's side until the very end.

Name: Uta Uta no Mi

Model Classification: Special Paramecia (Substance Generation, Environmental Manipulation)

Description: A fruit that weaponizes vocal resonance, allowing the user to generate physical musical notes and "carve" a space-time dome known as the Sing-World.

Weakness:

  • ⦿ Standard Devil Fruit weaknesses apply (Seastone/Water).
  • ⦿ Acoustic Isolation: Blocking Sound or preventing the user from singing nullifies all effects.
  • ⦿ Willpower Gap: Targets 50% stronger than the user are immune to environmental manipulation.
  • ⦿ Sensory Immunity: A Deaf target is immune to the Fruit's Environmental Manipulation abilities.
