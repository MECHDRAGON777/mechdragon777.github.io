Name: Mori Mori No Mi

Model Classification: Natural Logia

Description: The Mori Mori No Mi is a Logia-type Devil Fruit that allows the user to create, control, and transform into nature at will. It possesses extreme offensive and defensive power, capable of manipulating terrain and plant life. Its attacks can drain the liquid out of an individuals body.

Weakness:

  • Standard Devil Fruit weaknesses apply: Seastone and submersion in water.
  • Armament Haki and Sea-Prism weapons bypass Logia properties.
  • Since it’s based of plant life and nature this fruit is highly weak to fire and flame based techniques.
