【 𝐎𝐕𝐄𝐑𝐕𝐈𝐄𝐖 】 (概要, Gaiya) Training in the Kaigan universe is essential for characters to increase their power, refine their techniques, and achieve new masteries or haki. Unlike the orthodox methods, training in Kaigan can involve extreme physical exertion, mental discipline, and even the use of advanced technology or otherworldly methods. Training is a central aspect of the series, as many characters undergo intense sessions to push their limits and attain greater power to face increasingly formidable enemies. Training isn't just about physical exertions; it's also about mastering new fighting techniques, increasing [Doriki], and learning how to tap into one's willpower for devastating effects. This system will explain all the different types of gains you can make from our progression system! ◆ - A training session is logged by submitting a Progress Log, you can find this in Templates ◆ - (Note: The better you fill out your progress-log, the faster you receive approval, You might also get some extra gains maybe....)

【 𝐓𝐑𝐀𝐈𝐍𝐈𝐍𝐆 】 (進捗, Shinchoku) As mentioned above, training in Kaigan universe is essential for growth of the character. This post will contain different methods of training that are straightforward and direct in terms of involvement. These usually do not require anyone else and can be done by yourself at any given time. Note that training can be done towards even profession skills, it would involve you spending time to research, practice and develop your skills. ◆ - ❝Light Training❞ (+25) These trainings have little to no exertions on you, they are usually to warm-up and get in that little bit extra work here and there. [REQUIREMENTS] 1-2 Paragraphs ◆ - ❝Medium Training❞ (+40) While they may be challenging and force you to get serious, they are usually do not push you towards true exertions, medium trainings are essential to take a little recovery break, but still get the work in. [REQUIREMENTS] 2-3 Paragraphs ◆ - ❝Heavy Training❞ (+60) Heavy training usually involves high levels of intensity, pushing the individual to work hard and perform exercises that test their physical limits. At times capable of exhausting you. [REQUIREMENTS] 4-5 Paragraphs [TRAININGS CAN BE DONE ONLY 3X A WEEK] ◆ - ❝Serious Sparring❞ (+80) A serious spar is a full-fledged fight unlike an exhibition match, here you can hurt your opponent and land injuries, however it is not too serious to the point it gives a life-threatening injury. [REQUIREMENTS] +4-5 Quality Turns

The battles are typically set against powerful foes or a clash of beliefs. These battles are life-threatening and can result in Mortal Wounds or Death. ◆ - ❝Skirmish Battle❞ (1.1x) A prudent battle is one characterized by careful planning, strategic thinking, and measured actions. Unlike a reckless or impulsive confrontation, a prudent battle involves fighters who prioritize caution, foresight, and minimizing unnecessary risks. The focus is on making smart decisions, using tactics to outsmart or outmaneuver the opponent, and seeking victory without overextending or making costly mistakes. 【 𝐋𝐞𝐭𝐡𝐚𝐥 𝐁𝐚𝐭𝐭𝐥𝐞 】 (戦い, Shobu) A lethal battle refers to a confrontation where the stakes are life and death, and the outcome directly impacts the survival of the participants. In a lethal battle, both sides are often willing to use their most destructive techniques, with the intention to defeat or eliminate their opponent. The environment is typically tense, brutal, and chaotic, with significant consequences for failure. This can be gatherings, bondings, conversations and as much as adventuring something. Anything that requires two characters personalities to interact falls under this category: ◆ - ❝Casual Adventure❞ (+25) Casual adventures are typically light, informal, and brief. They often happen in everyday settings, without much emotional investment or a deep connection. Casual conversations tend to stay on the surface level, focusing on general topics that don't delve into personal feelings or experiences. They are often brief and non-intimate. ◆ - ❝Meaningful Adventure❞ (+50) Meaningful interactions are deeper and more intentional. They often involve sharing personal thoughts, feelings, or experiences, and there is typically an emotional or intellectual exchange. These adventures usually have a greater purpose, such as building a relationship, offering support, or gaining a deeper understanding of each other. They often lead to stronger connections and can contribute to character growth or mutual understanding. ◆ - ❝Errand❞ (+50k-325k Beli) One can now go around and practice their profession for money, this means that you can help civilians around, make deals or simply help out parties for a bunch of money.
